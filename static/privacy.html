1. Commitment to Privacy

Astra is built with privacy and data isolation as core principles. This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal data, uploaded documents, conversation history, and technical telemetry are gathered, processed, secured, and purged.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information to provide the secure RAG AI service:

  • Account Credentials: Email address, username, phone number (if provided), and cryptographic password hash (Argon2id). We never store raw passwords.
  • Uploaded Documents: Text, PDF, DOCX, and other files you explicitly upload for knowledge retrieval.
  • Conversations & Queries: Prompts submitted to the assistant and synthesized responses. Temporary chat mode is available for ephemerality.
  • Technical Metadata: Query response latencies, model telemetry, and error logs for platform health and abuse prevention.

3. Strict Multi-User Isolation

Astra enforces strict server-side authorization: User A can never view, query, or access User B's documents, conversations, or embeddings. Vector embeddings and document stores are segregated by authenticated user identifier in both relational databases and vector indices.

4. Zero Sale of Personal Data

We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, documents, or conversation records to advertisers, data brokers, or external entities.

5. Secure Cookie & Token Architecture

Astra uses modern stateless authentication:

  • Authentication tokens are transmitted via secure HTTP headers and HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax cookies.
  • Client JavaScript cannot manipulate or expose core authentication secrets.
  • AI provider API keys are strictly maintained on the server-side environment and are never transmitted to or accessible by client browsers.

6. Right to Deletion & Data Portability

You have full ownership of your data and the absolute right to delete your account at any time:

  • You may visit our dedicated Account Deletion Page to permanently erase your profile, all uploaded documents, vector embeddings, and conversation histories.
  • Deletion is instantaneous and irreversible.

7. Contact & Privacy Inquiries

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please email: privacy@astra.local.
